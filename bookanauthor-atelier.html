Roles

How Atelier is structured

Buyer

Read and host

Create a free account. Buy print or classroom sets. Request an in-person or virtual visit. Pay in one checkout. Track orders and bookings.

Author

Sell and appear

List books with price and stock. Publish visit packages (format, duration, travel radius, fee). Accept or decline requests. Withdraw earnings after admin clearance.

Super admin

Trust layer

Approve new authors and listings. Suspend abuse. View every order and booking. Adjust fees. The marketplace does not go live without this desk.
